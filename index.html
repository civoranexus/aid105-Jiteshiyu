SchemeAssist AI

An intelligent system that helps citizens discover government schemes they are actually eligible for.

The Problem

Millions of citizens miss out on government welfare schemes due to lack of awareness and unclear eligibility criteria.

The Solution

SchemeAssist AI analyzes citizen profiles and recommends suitable schemes using transparent eligibility logic and AI-driven insights.

Key Features

  • Eligibility-based scheme recommendations
  • Clear and explainable results
