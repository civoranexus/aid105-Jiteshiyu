# SchemeAssist AI

SchemeAssist AI is an independent, AI-assisted civic technology platform designed
to help citizens discover government welfare schemes they may be eligible for,
based on structured eligibility criteria and explainable guidance.

## Problem Statement
Many government welfare schemes remain underutilized due to:
- Lack of awareness
- Complex eligibility requirements
- Fragmented information across multiple portals

## Solution Overview
SchemeAssist AI addresses this problem by:
- Organizing scheme information around eligibility criteria
- Providing structured, rule-based matching
- Using AI to explain eligibility conditions clearly
