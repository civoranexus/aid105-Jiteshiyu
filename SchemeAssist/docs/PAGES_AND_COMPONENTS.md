# Pages and Components

## Core Pages
- Home: Introduces the platform, problem, and approach
- About: Explains methodology, AI usage, and independence
- Schemes: Reserved for scheme listing and filtering
- Disclaimer: Defines scope, non-affiliation, and liability limits
- Privacy Policy: Explains data handling and AI processing
- Terms of Use: Defines acceptable usage and user responsibilities

## Core Components
- Navbar: Primary navigation and platform identity
- Footer: Legal links, trust signals, and platform context

## Design Considerations
- Neutral, institutional tone
- Accessibility-friendly structure
- No overstatement of AI capabilities
