# Project Overview – SchemeAssist AI

SchemeAssist AI is a public-interest software project focused on improving
access to government welfare scheme information.

The platform is designed as a guidance system, not an official application
or approval portal.

## Key Design Principles
- Eligibility-first discovery
- Transparency and explainability
- Minimal data collection
- Non-affiliation with government authorities
- AI used for assistance, not decision-making

## Target Users
- Citizens seeking welfare scheme information
- Students and researchers studying civic technology
- Demonstration and academic evaluation purposes
